## Decisions made so far

- **Framing question** for the whole course, asked at the start and revisited at the end: What is creativity? What is taste? Can taste be generated?
- **Principle:** keep going back and forth between the big abstract picture and down-to-earth practice.
- **Structure (draft):** 4 sessions. What is this thing? / How does it work? / Moving images and space / AI beyond the tools, and back to taste.
- **Demos (approved):** 1. photo edit in ChatGPT, 2. sketch or massing to many renders, 3. render to walkthrough video, 4. full pipeline from brief to pitch, maybe plus a live coding-agent build.
- **Taste exercise:** "Pick one." Generate 12 to 20 variations, everyone votes silently, show the spread, ask why. Fits at the end of Demo 2.
- **Kept ideas:** the etymology hook (*inter-legere*, "to choose between"), AlphaGo's move 37, the kinds-of-intelligence slide (fungi, octopus, and ants are the keepers).
- **Dropped:** the blind test, the "Whose taste is this?" guessing game, and slime mold.
- **Videos:** the two Anthropic videos on how Claude thinks (links in IDEAS.md).
